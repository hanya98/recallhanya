## Section 1 — The moment we fix

**Eyebrow**
The browser is a memory leak.

**Headline**
You saw it. You just can't find it.

**Body**
You open 50 tabs a day. You bookmark maybe one. A week later you remember the *idea* — "the React auth library I was reading about" — but not the URL.

Browser history gives you a timestamp. Bookmarks give you a folder. Search engines give you the public web.

None of them remember *your* stream of it.

RecallOS does.
