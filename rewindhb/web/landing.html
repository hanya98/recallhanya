Privacy by default

Your memory is yours.

We don't believe in trading your data for product. RecallOS runs on a hard rule: if it's not on your device or your account, we don't have it.

  • No raw HTML stored. Only title, URL, AI summary, and tags.
  • End-to-end private. Never shared with other users. Never used for training.
  • Pause anytime. One toggle in the extension popup stops capture instantly.
  • One-click delete. Any memory, any time. Hard-deleted after 30 days.
